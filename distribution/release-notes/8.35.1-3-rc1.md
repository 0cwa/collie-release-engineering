## Collie 8.35.1-3 RC1

- **Settings are easier to navigate.** Network controls now come first in Privacy, chat export comes last in Chats, and donation links sit above Help. Removed the toolbar donation item and corrected the Help branding.
- **Collie artwork in the status bar and locked startup screen.** The normal dark background remains `#24130D`.
- **Notification readiness follows your selected push provider.** A stale token from another provider no longer makes push appear ready.
- **Smoother recipient settings and safer service startup.** Settings update while linked-device information loads, and locked services stop unnecessary startup work earlier.
- **Clearer source history and release records.** Corrected generated commit dates, added verified signatures, and streamlined release verification.

Thanks to [Miha Zupan](https://github.com/MihaZupan) for the Molly implementations adapted for push readiness, locked services, and recipient settings.
