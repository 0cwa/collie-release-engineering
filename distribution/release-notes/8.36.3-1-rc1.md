## Collie 8.36.3-1 RC1

- **Updated to Signal 8.36.3.** This release candidate brings in upstream fixes for conversations, media, and backup restoration.
- **Signal donation checkout removed.** Collie removes the in-app checkout and donation management screens. Backup subscriptions, previously received gifts, and the external Collie and Molly support links remain.
- **Group privacy.** A blocked contact who invites you to a group can no longer trigger automatic profile sharing through the inviter trust check.
- **Attachments and backups.** Uploads stay bound to the attachment bytes selected for sending. If backup import setup fails, Collie leaves any transaction opened by the caller alone. Restore response details stay out of diagnostic logs.
